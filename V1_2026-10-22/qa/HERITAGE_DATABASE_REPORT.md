## Display model

Featured is eligibility for the curated map marker layer, not a promise to render 221 pins simultaneously. Existing clustering/camera logic remains. Listed records are available in country/province/district inventories, search, canonical pages and custom tours without forcing extra permanent pins. Search, designation and category filters, A–Z ordering and 24-record paging keep large scoped inventories usable.

All accepted Places have generated canonical pages and Add to custom tour. Article, Tour, Event and Project connections are derived from authored stable-ID relationships, with empty sections hidden. Component metadata and original coordinates are not rewritten to fit geography.
