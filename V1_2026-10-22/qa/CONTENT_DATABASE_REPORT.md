## Quality method and limits

The machine audit checks unique title/slug/keyword/meta-description, valid category and Place IDs, structured headings, reference metadata, Review/provenance flags, prose length, substantive repeated paragraphs and pairwise 5-gram overlap. Thresholds are Jaccard≥0.12 or smaller-document containment≥0.25. This is a duplication screen, not proof of historical correctness or a comprehensive plagiarism service.

Article-specific research notes are retained under archive/research-a, research-b and research-c. Source conflicts, access limits and unverified tradition are disclosed rather than silently resolved. Full factual/editorial review remains necessary before publication. Bibliographic metadata is not claimed as a full-text reading; source-image access is not media permission.

ARCHIVE_CONTENT_PLAN.csv records the editorial plan; ARCHIVE_CONTENT_QUALITY.csv reports actual drafted records. See archive/CORPUS_QUALITY.json for every result and warning. Body-free Archive discovery was separately tested at 100/250/500 synthetic records, with 12 cards per page.
