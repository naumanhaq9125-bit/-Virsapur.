## Evidence and editorial limits

Source-specific access limits remain in each record’s internalNotes. Research used institutional and primary pages, selected document passages, indexed excerpts and catalogue metadata; not every full publication was accessible. In particular, some large PDFs exceeded the reader limit, some journal PDFs returned 403, and some dynamic pages exposed only indexed text. The batch QA wording now states this explicitly.

No copied passages or captions were used. Source-derived prose was kept within conservative page-level budgets, including reused sources; original analytical examples are not presented as field observations. Per-record estimates are research notes, not an automated plagiarism certification. Human fact-checking and source assessment remain required before publication.
