## Distinctness and connections

IDs, slugs, working titles and primary keywords are unique across all 420 rows. The plan uses only the existing six category IDs. Topic families are an editorial planning field, not new public categories.

The plan was checked against the integrated **606 canonical Places**. It links 320 articles to 204 distinct canonical heritage IDs; the other 100 are legitimately thematic, linguistic, literary, material or biographical concepts without an exact current Place relationship. No Place IDs were invented. All linked district and province IDs come from the current geographic assignments; cross-regional commissions are not forced into one district. Taxila remains one existing serial property and is not split into invented Places.

Place-led introductions are distinguished from focused commissions on architectural detail, archaeological methods, objects, cultural practice and regional context. For example, the existing Moenjodaro wells, lanes, architecture and Great Bath topics retain separate scopes; a city-orientation concept is not a substitute for those articles. Katas Raj and Satgraha use the same canonical Place intentionally, while addressing ensemble orientation and a component-level architectural question respectively.

## Source and drafting gate

There are 248 distinct institutional or scholarly source URLs across the individual source plans, including the canonical Place provenance. Source discovery is **not** a claim that all 420 proposed articles are fully researched. Some source routes are institutional catalogues or programme indexes: they must lead to relevant records, publications, authenticated editions or interviews before factual prose is drafted. A general institutional source must never be stretched to support unverified specifics.

The Pakistan Academy of Letters awards document is explicitly a research finder, not proof of an author's biography or a poem's text. Literary articles need identified editions, translations and author-specific references. The national intangible-heritage register was indexed but the full document exceeded the web reader limit; page-level content still needs checking. Community language and practice articles require appropriate community-led sources and care with contested labels. Present-day access, ownership, conservation status and activity cannot be inferred from older documents.

One preserved legacy concept, **Gurdwara Lahura Sahib**, is explicitly held for identity and location verification. The existing ID and slug are retained, but no district or heritage relationship is invented. Anarkali's catalogue coordinate is disputed; the plan uses the building as an editorial subject without introducing a map point. Khewra has official district and operator research routes but no invented canonical Place link.

Instagram searches and attempted public profile retrieval did not establish usable post-level material. No Instagram caption, image or ownership claim is used as evidence. The plan preserves the 12 available Virsapur story ideas; no standalone reel-script file was found in the repository search. This limitation is explicit rather than presented as a completed account review.

**No articles have been drafted, no CSV has been written, and no canonical content has been changed by this planning task.** The root agent must first produce the requested CSV and explicitly acknowledge the plan before article drafting begins. After that gate, each draft still requires source-level verification, differentiated original prose and review status; word counts alone do not establish editorial completion.
