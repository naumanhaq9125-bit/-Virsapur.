Training College for Men Tilak Incline

Type: Monument - Building
Province: Sindh
District: Hyderabad
Period: Historic
Relative Chronology: 1857 - 1947 CE
Description: This is a double storey college building of burnt bricks masonry. The building has a wide corridor, arched windows and open halls. It was constructed in 1854 CE.
Latitude: 25.389058333
Longitude: 68.368155556
Ownership: Private
Legal Status: Not Protected
Title of Publication: Inventory of Cultural Property in the Province of Sindh, Pakistan, 2015
Published In: Government of Sindh, Vol 1
Year of Publication: 2015
Bibliography/Reference: Jansen, Michael, Yasmeen Lari
