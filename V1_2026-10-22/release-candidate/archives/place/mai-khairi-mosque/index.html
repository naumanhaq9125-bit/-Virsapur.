Mai Khairi Mosque

Heritage Site

Tando Allahyar · Sindh

Mai Khairi mosque at Nasarpur is a modest brick prayer hall with an adjoining courtyard. Its decorative tiled floor and mihrab panels preserve a characteristic use of Kashi ceramics in Sindhi sacred architecture.
