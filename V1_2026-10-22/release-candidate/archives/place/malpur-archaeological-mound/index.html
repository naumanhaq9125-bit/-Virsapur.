Malpur Archaeological Mound

Heritage Site

Islamabad · Islamabad Capital Territory

The mound near Malpur contains settlement traces overlooking the Rawal Lake landscape. Pottery, a stone pestle and a coin recorded by the survey provide material evidence of earlier occupation.
