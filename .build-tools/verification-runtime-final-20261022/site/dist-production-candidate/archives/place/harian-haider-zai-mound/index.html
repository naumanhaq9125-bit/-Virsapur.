Harian Haider Zai Mound

Heritage site

Loralai · Balochistan

A broad prehistoric mound near Harian Haider Zai contains evidence of settlement recorded through pottery and stone tools. The inventory connects this landscape with archaeological investigations around Loralai; the mound is retained as a distinct surveyed location.
