Historic Monument · Bahawalpur

Noor Mahal

Major heritage site

Bahawalpur · Punjab

A palace of the former Bahawalpur princely state with a neoclassical architectural composition. Its nineteenth-century design reflects the court’s engagement with European building traditions.
