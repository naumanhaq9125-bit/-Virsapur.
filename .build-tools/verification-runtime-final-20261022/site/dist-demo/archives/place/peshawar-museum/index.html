Peshawar Museum

Major Heritage Site

Peshawar · Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Peshawar Museum houses important Gandharan sculpture alongside collections reflecting later regional history. The museum's early-twentieth-century building is itself part of the city's institutional heritage.
