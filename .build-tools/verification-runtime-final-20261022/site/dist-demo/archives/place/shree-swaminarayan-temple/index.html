Shree Swaminarayan Temple

Heritage Site

South Karachi · Sindh

This historic Karachi temple belongs to the Nar Narayan Dev Gadi tradition of the Swaminarayan community. It preserves a built place of Hindu worship within the city’s diverse religious landscape.
