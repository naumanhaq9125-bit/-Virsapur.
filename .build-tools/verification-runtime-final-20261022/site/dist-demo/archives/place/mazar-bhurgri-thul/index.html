Mazar Bhurgri Thul

Heritage Site

Dadu · Sindh

The Mazar Bhurgri thul preserves a raised brick mass with moulded brick fragments and pottery around it. The survey identifies the monument with Sindh’s Buddhist archaeological tradition while recording substantial erosion.
