Historic Monument · Chiniot

Gulzar Mahal, Chiniot

Major heritage site

Chiniot · Punjab

An early twentieth-century mansion celebrated for carved woodwork, elaborate ceilings and stucco decoration. Its interiors bring together several of Chiniot’s building crafts.
