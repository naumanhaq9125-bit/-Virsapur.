Tomb of Tahir Khan Nahar

Major heritage site

Muzaffargarh · Punjab

An octagonal, tiered mausoleum at Sitpur, with sloping walls and glazed-tile decoration. Its form reflects the regional funerary architecture associated with Multan and the lower Indus plain.
