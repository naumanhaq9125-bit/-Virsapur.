Victoria Clock Tower, Jacobabad

Major Heritage Site

Jacobabad · Sindh

Jacobabad’s Victoria Clock Tower is a colonial-period civic landmark constructed in brick and lime. Its arched rooms and internal stair express the layered design of a public clock-tower building.
