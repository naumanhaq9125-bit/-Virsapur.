Akra Archaeological Mounds

Major Heritage Site

Bannu · Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

The mounds at Akra preserve evidence of a long-lived settlement in the Bannu basin. Pottery, sculpture fragments and coins recorded here connect the site with successive ancient and medieval occupations.
