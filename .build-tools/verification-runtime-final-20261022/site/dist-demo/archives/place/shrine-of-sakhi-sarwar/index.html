Shrine of Sakhi Sarwar

Major heritage site

Dera Ghazi Khan · Punjab

A mausoleum complex in the Sulaiman foothills associated with the saint Sakhi Sarwar. It represents an important sacred place in the heritage landscape west of Dera Ghazi Khan.
