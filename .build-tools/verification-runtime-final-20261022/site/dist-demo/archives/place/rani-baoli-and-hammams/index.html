Rani Baoli and Hammams

Major heritage site

Sudhnoti · Azad Jammu and Kashmir

An enclosed bathing complex at Palandri contains stepped water tanks and adjoining chambers. Dressed-stone work and arched entrances preserve historic water and bathing architecture.
