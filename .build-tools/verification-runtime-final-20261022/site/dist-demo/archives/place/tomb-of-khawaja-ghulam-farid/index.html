Tomb of Khawaja Ghulam Farid

Major heritage site

Rajanpur · Punjab

A domed mausoleum associated with the poet Khawaja Ghulam Farid. Glazed tiles, frescoes, inscriptions and floral ornament distinguish the tomb and adjoining structures.
