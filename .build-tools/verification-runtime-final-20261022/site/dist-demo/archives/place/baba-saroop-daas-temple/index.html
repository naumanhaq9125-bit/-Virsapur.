Baba Saroop Daas Temple

Major Heritage Site

Naushahro Feroze · Sindh

The temple of Baba Saroop Daas at Halani is a multi-storey brick complex with a domed feature and an enclosing wall. It is a surviving example of Sindh’s Hindu sacred architecture.
