Historic Monument · Islamabad

Sarai Kharbuza

Major heritage site

Islamabad · Islamabad Capital Territory

A historic caravanserai preserves arched gateways, rows of accommodation cells and a mosque. Its brick construction records the infrastructure of travel along an older route through the Islamabad area.
