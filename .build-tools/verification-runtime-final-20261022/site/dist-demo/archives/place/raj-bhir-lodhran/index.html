Raj Bhir, Lodhran

Major heritage site

Lodhran · Punjab

A settlement mound in the Lodhran region with a substantial surface pottery assemblage. The survey records applied and stamped ceramic decoration.

What a pottery assemblage can say about a settlement

A pottery assemblage reveals patterns that an isolated decorated vessel cannot. Recording fabric, form, quantity and context helps distinguish evidence of making, use and deposition from attractive speculation.
