Ranjit Singh Birthplace Haveli

Major heritage site

Gujranwala · Punjab

A historic mansion in Gujranwala associated with Maharaja Ranjit Singh’s birth. Its reception hall, courtyards and decorated facade preserve a substantial domestic ensemble.
