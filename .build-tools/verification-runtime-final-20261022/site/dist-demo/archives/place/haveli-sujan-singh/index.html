Historic Urban Heritage · Rawalpindi

Haveli Sujan Singh

Major heritage site

Rawalpindi · Punjab

A late nineteenth-century mansion organised around an open courtyard in Rawalpindi. Ornate entrances, halls and upper-storey rooms preserve evidence of the city’s historic residential architecture.
