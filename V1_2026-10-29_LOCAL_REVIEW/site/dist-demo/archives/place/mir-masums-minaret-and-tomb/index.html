Mir Masum’s Minaret and Tomb

Major Heritage Site

Sukkur · Sindh

Mir Masum’s tall brick minaret and family tombs form a major Mughal-period monument in Sukkur. The complex combines an internal staircase, domed summit and inscribed funerary canopies.
