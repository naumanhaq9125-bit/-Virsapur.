Wooden Mosque, Kalam

Major Heritage Site

Swat · Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Kalam's wooden mosque is constructed with deodar timber and decorated with floral and geometric carving. Its two-storey form represents the distinctive timber-building traditions of the upper Swat valley.
