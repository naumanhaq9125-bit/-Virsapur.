Chanhudaro

Major Heritage Site

Shaheed Benazir Abad · Sindh

Chanhudaro is an Indus-period settlement whose excavations revealed brick houses, drainage and varied craft objects. Its surviving mounds are an important archaeological record of settlement and production in Sindh.
