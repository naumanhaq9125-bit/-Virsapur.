Tomb of Ghazi Khan

Major heritage site

Dera Ghazi Khan · Punjab

An octagonal brick mausoleum in Dera Ghazi Khan with tapering stages and decorative turrets. Tile mosaic links its architecture to the region’s funerary traditions.
