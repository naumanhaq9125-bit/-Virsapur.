Vijnot Jo Daro

Major Heritage Site

Ghotki · Sindh

Vijnot Jo Daro is an extensive archaeological mound complex in the Deharki area. Pottery, brick fragments and charcoal documented in the official survey preserve evidence of an earlier settlement whose chronology remains cautiously attributed.
