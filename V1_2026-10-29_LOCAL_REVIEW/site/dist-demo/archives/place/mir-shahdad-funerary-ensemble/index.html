Mir Shahdad Funerary Ensemble

Heritage Site

Shaheed Benazir Abad · Sindh

The funerary ensemble at Shahpur includes carved stone tombs, brick mausoleums and mosques. Inscriptions, painted surfaces and finely worked grave markers illustrate several phases of Sindh’s elite funerary architecture.
