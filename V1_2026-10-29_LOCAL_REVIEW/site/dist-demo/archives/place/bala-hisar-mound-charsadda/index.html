Bala Hisar Mound, Charsadda

Major Heritage Site

Charsadda · Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Excavations at this mound revealed a long sequence of occupation associated with ancient Pushkalavati. It is an important source for understanding settlement and urban life in Gandhara.
