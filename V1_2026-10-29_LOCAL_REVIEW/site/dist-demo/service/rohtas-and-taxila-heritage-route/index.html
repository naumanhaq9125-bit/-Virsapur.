Overview

A multi-day planning route linking Rohtas Fort with Taxila’s archaeological landscape, starting from Lahore and shaped through a personal quote.

Rohtas Fort and Taxila support different ways of understanding the northern Punjab landscape. The fort invites close attention to walls, gateways and the organisation of a defensible place. Taxila is a serial archaeological property whose settlements and religious sites should be understood together, not reduced to a single map pin or monument. This route therefore treats Taxila as one canonical Heritage Place with multiple documented components. A proposed visit would identify which components suit the available time and access conditions; adding Taxila does not promise entry to every component. Travel begins from the configured Virsapur origin in Lahore. Transport, overnight arrangements, daily travel distances and site permissions must be assessed before the itinerary is agreed. The page does not advertise a fixed departure, package price or guaranteed opening hours. Use the Heritage records below as a research starting point, then request a custom quote for the places and pace your group prefers.
