Overview

A proposed journey connecting Moenjodaro, Makli and Shah Jahan Mosque, with travel from Lahore and practical arrangements reviewed individually.

This planning route brings together an archaeological city, a major funerary landscape and a living mosque without presenting them as one continuous historical episode. Moenjodaro asks questions about urban organisation and the interpretation of excavated remains. Makli offers a different encounter with monuments, burial traditions and remembered political communities around Thatta. Shah Jahan Mosque introduces another architectural and devotional setting. Each Place has its own source record and designation; the mosque is not described here as an inscribed World Heritage property. The proposed sequence is an editorial framework for a conversation, not a bookable scheduled departure. Starting from Lahore, the final route depends on transport options, seasonal conditions, access permissions, group needs and the time available. No price is calculated on this page. A custom quote should identify the selected places and practical requirements so Virsapur can review feasibility before making any commitment. The related records below provide context while keeping the three places distinct.
