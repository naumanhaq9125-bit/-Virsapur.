Overview

A proposed route through Delhi Gate, Shahi Hammam and Wazir Khan Mosque, connecting an urban threshold, a historic bathhouse and a living sacred landmark.

This curated planning route approaches Lahore through the relationship between streets and institutions. Delhi Gate introduces the historic city's edge and the passage into its commercial fabric. The nearby Shahi Hammam provides a different setting for considering water, service and architectural conservation. Wazir Khan Mosque then brings the route into a major sacred and urban ensemble. These are three separate Heritage records, not interchangeable examples of a single monument type. The sequence is a starting point for discussion, not a confirmed guided departure. Virsapur will review current opening arrangements, worship requirements, walking conditions, group access and available interpretation before agreeing the final programme. Select the places below to examine their records, or start a custom itinerary and change the order to suit your group's interests.
