Bhong Mosque

Major heritage site

Rahim Yar Khan · Punjab

A richly ornamented twentieth-century mosque combining tile, painted, inlaid and carved decoration. Completed in 1982, the project received the Aga Khan Award for Architecture in the 1984–1986 cycle.
