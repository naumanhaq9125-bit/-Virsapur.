Archaeological Site · Sheikhupura

Mian Ali Faqiran Archaeological Mound

Major heritage site

Sheikhupura · Punjab

An excavated settlement mound with brick walls, a well and layered occupation deposits. Federal investigations recovered substantial material evidence of the site’s long history.
