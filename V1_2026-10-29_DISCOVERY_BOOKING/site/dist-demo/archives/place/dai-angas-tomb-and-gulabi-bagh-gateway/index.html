Dai Anga’s Tomb and Gulabi Bagh Gateway

Major heritage site

Lahore · Punjab

A domed brick mausoleum and decorated garden gateway form a historic ensemble beside Lahore’s old Grand Trunk Road. Tile mosaic and calligraphy distinguish the surviving structures.
