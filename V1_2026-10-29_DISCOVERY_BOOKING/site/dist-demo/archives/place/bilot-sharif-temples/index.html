Bilot Sharif Temples

Major Heritage Site

D. I. Khan · Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

A fortified enclosure at Bilot Sharif contains a group of dressed-stone Hindu temples. The surviving temples and defensive walls illustrate the region's religious and architectural heritage.

Bilot Sharif Temples and the stone-building tradition

Bilot Sharif’s temples belong to a regional stone-building tradition whose chronology cannot be reduced to one dynasty. Their materials, arrangements and changing scholarly labels reward careful comparison.
