Sacred Heritage · Lahore

Data Darbar

Major heritage site

Lahore · Punjab

The mausoleum complex of Ali Hujwiri in Lahore includes marble-clad sacred spaces and a large courtyard. Its present architecture reflects successive expansion and renewal.
