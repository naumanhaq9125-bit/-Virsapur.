Baba Khatawara Ashram, Gandhawa

Heritage site

Jhal Magsi · Balochistan

A Hindu ashram in Gandhawa’s old bazaar includes a temple, assembly space and residential rooms. Surviving decorative details and the arrangement of the compound preserve evidence of the town’s historic religious and community life.
