Archaeological Site · Bahawalpur

Ganweriwala

Major heritage site

Bahawalpur · Punjab

Two extensive mounds in Cholistan preserve remains associated with the Mature Harappan phase. The site is important to the archaeological study of settlement across the desert landscape.
