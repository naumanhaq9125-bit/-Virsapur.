Historic Urban Heritage · Rawalpindi

Khem Singh Bedi Haveli

Major heritage site

Rawalpindi · Punjab

A multi-storey haveli with courtyards, verandas and projecting oriel windows. The survey documents extensive frescoes across its rooms, pillars and facade.
