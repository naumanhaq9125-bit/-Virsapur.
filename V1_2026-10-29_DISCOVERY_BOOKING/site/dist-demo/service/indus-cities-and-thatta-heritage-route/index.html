Overview

A four-day journey connecting Moenjodaro, Makli and Shah Jahan Mosque, with travel from Lahore.

This planning route brings together an archaeological city, a major funerary landscape and a living mosque without presenting them as one continuous historical episode. Moenjodaro asks questions about urban organisation and the interpretation of excavated remains. Makli offers a different encounter with monuments, burial traditions and remembered political communities around Thatta. Shah Jahan Mosque introduces another architectural and devotional setting. Each Place has its own source record and designation; the mosque is not described here as an inscribed World Heritage property. The fixed journey brings these three places together, with travel from Lahore and practical arrangements shared for each departure. The related records below provide context while keeping the three places distinct.
