Overview

A two-day heritage journey linking Rohtas Fort with Taxila’s archaeological landscape, departing from Lahore.

Rohtas Fort and Taxila support different ways of understanding the northern Punjab landscape. The fort invites close attention to walls, gateways and the organisation of a defensible place. Taxila is a serial archaeological property whose settlements and religious sites should be understood together, not reduced to a single map pin or monument. This route therefore treats Taxila as one canonical Heritage Place with multiple documented components. The fixed itinerary visits selected components of Taxila alongside Rohtas Fort. Travel begins in Lahore; transport, overnight arrangements and practical site access form part of the departure information.
