# Virsapur Homepage Visual-Direction Reference Notes

Reviewed 25 August 2026. These notes concern information design and composition only; no reference branding, proprietary illustration or page layout is copied.

## National Trust UK

Source: https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/

- The main discovery task is explicit and early: “Find a place to visit” combines a location input with an immediate Explore action. Virsapur can adapt the same priority by placing Heritage Map discovery before institutional explanation.
- The navigation decomposes a broad “visit” intent into three useful lenses—area, thing to do and place type—rather than asking visitors to understand the organisation first. Virsapur’s quick-discovery bar similarly separates place, experience, learning and stories.
- Current content is not isolated in a news archive. “What’s on” appears beside the place-finding journey, so a future Virsapur current-item module should render date, location and type as practical discovery metadata.
- Content cards move between family activities, day-out ideas, learning and preservation work. The changing subject/scale creates rhythm while retaining consistent card anatomy.
- The cause and membership story appears after useful visitor discovery. Virsapur’s short About composition should therefore follow the map and experiences.

## VisitBritain

Source: https://www.visitbritain.com/en

- The homepage opens with one campaign proposition and one action, supported immediately by atmospheric photography and location credit. Virsapur’s hero should likewise use one place-rich image and only two decision paths.
- A campaign can flow directly into a thematic interactive map; the map is presented as a way to enter real places and experiences, not as technical infrastructure. Virsapur should frame its map as “Explore Pakistan.”
- “Best things to see and do” mixes timely editorial, events, itineraries and attraction content in image-led cards. Virsapur’s future current-content record should support several types without requiring a different component per type.
- Cards expose location and image credit close to the image. Virsapur should retain structured location, caption and credit fields even when they are not yet complete.
- Regional itineraries use repeatable cards but let strong photography and concise action language carry most of the composition; descriptive copy remains secondary.

## Visit Jordan

Source: https://international.visitjordan.com/

- Primary navigation distinguishes Discover, Experiences and Attractions, helping visitors choose between place-led and activity-led exploration. Virsapur adapts this distinction in its header and quick-discovery bar.
- The hero proposition is short and atmospheric (“kingdom of time”), followed by a compact invitation to explore; image and tone establish destination before detail.
- Major discovery cards combine a strong place/experience name, a short evocative line and one repeated “Explore now” action. Virsapur experience cards should use the same low-copy, high-image discipline.
- The site groups experience types such as history/culture, adventure, leisure and faith separately from named attractions such as Petra or the Dead Sea. Virsapur can later distinguish programme type from geographic relationship in the CMS.
- Practical trip essentials are grouped into a compact utility cluster instead of being mixed into every storytelling card. Virsapur’s programme detail schema already supports practical fields that can remain hidden until populated.

## Qatar Museums

Source: https://qm.org.qa/en/

- The top-level information architecture is action-based and compact: Stories, What’s On, Collections and Visit. Virsapur should keep its header similarly direct rather than exposing internal organisational structure.
- The opening experience surfaces individual venues through large photography and a focused “visit website” action. A network feels tangible because the places appear before the institutional narrative.
- “What’s On” uses type, exact date/time and venue as first-class card content, with quick date filters for All dates, Today and Next 7 days. Virsapur’s future current module should use those same data categories and remain hidden until records exist.
- Heritage sites are surfaced as a distinct visual collection after events, making the heritage remit concrete. Virsapur’s map and experience imagery should similarly demonstrate actual places rather than rely on mission statements.
- Audience shortcuts for families, schools and researchers appear as functional navigation. Virsapur should support audience taxonomy in the CMS, but should not display those claims until programme records contain approved audience data.

## Virsapur adaptation decisions

- Full-bleed Badshahi Mosque hero with two actions.
- Four-option discovery bar overlapping the hero edge.
- Heritage Map as the first substantial content module.
- Asymmetric, image-led experience grid with one large feature and three smaller cards.
- Current, story and project modules remain conditional on published records.
- Short photographic About composition with one embedded testimonial, followed by an image-backed final action.
- Institutional detail, founders and long-form editorial remain on their dedicated pages.
