# Virsapur Homepage Visual Direction Pass

## 1. What changed visually

- Replaced the pale split-layout homepage with a full-bleed, image-led Badshahi Mosque hero and a controlled ink/slate gradient.
- Simplified the header into a direct culture-and-discovery navigation with a single high-priority contact action and a full mobile menu.
- Added an overlapping four-path discovery bar so visitors can immediately reach the Heritage Map, tours, learning programmes, or Archives.
- Made the Heritage Map the homepage's signature discovery experience inside a strong slate section, with methodology kept in a discreet disclosure.
- Replaced text-heavy programme columns with an asymmetric image-card composition: one large experience and three supporting experiences.
- Reworked the About area as a photographic mosaic with concise institutional copy and one supporting quotation.
- Replaced the final pale text block with an image-backed closing action that offers experience and collaboration paths.
- Rebalanced EB Garamond and Inter, reduced border dependence, restored the natural colour of Virsapur photography, and introduced consistent primary, secondary, and text-action styles.
- Added restrained card/image/arrow interaction states and reduced-motion support.

## 4. Reference-site patterns adapted

- National Trust UK: place-first discovery, prominent visit planning, and current-content modules that disappear cleanly when there is nothing publishable.
- VisitBritain: atmospheric campaign-style hero imagery, practical discovery shortcuts, varied visual-card scale, and a map as a primary exploration path.
- Visit Jordan: clear separation of places, experiences, and attractions; concise destination-led cards; practical action language.
- Qatar Museums: direct action navigation, image-led venue/heritage cards, compact institutional explanation, and published activity modules with useful metadata.

The patterns were adapted to Virsapur's content and brand rather than copied as layouts or branding.

## 6. Missing Virsapur photography/content

- There are no verified published current events, walks, workshops, courses, or campaigns, so the What's Happening module is intentionally hidden.
- There are no publishable homepage story records or project records, so the Stories and Our Work previews are intentionally hidden rather than filled with invented content.
- The existing archive supplies authentic architecture, archaeological-site, object, fresco, and landscape imagery, but the intended travel/culture direction would improve further with original photographs of people experiencing sites, guided tours, workshops, schools, practitioners, cultural events, streets, and living heritage.
- Future programme photography should be captured specifically for Heritage Tours, Workshops, Research/Documentation, and Cultural Events so each category has a distinct, contemporary visual identity.
